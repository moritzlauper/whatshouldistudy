Advanced General Dental Practice (M.Sc.)

Profile

Place of study
International Medical College IMC® (Muenster, Germany), University of Duisburg-Essen (Essen, Germany) and various other training places.
Start date
  • 3 semesters (18 Months): Start on 1 April of the year, Ends in October of the following year
  • 4 semesters (24 months): Start on 1 October of the year, Ends in October of the year after next
Duration

The programme comprises 1500 hours (3 semesters) or 2250 hours (4 semesters).

Degree

Master of Science in Advanced General Dental Practice (M.Sc.).

Admission

admission-free
(please note possible access requirements)

Preliminary note

This degree program is being phased out. Enrolment in the variant with 90 ECTS is possible until the end of the enrolment period of the winter semester 2024/2025.

Description

This master course is an additional qualification for licensed dentists to deepen and intensify all fields of practical dentistry. Please note, that for to work as a dentist in Germany you have to do the equivalency exam to get the German approbation at the local government. Different regulations apply to the equivalence exam for different countries of origin.

The International Medical College IMC® performs postgraduate international Master's courses using a blended learning concept; theoretical contents (basic course and specialization course) are conveyed through our e-learning platform e-med via the internet, together with practical clinical courses, advanced training, lectures and seminars in our own virtual conference system. The basic course and the specialization course concludes with an exam. In addition, the students have to write a master thesis and to pass the final oral exam.

Course of study

Course of study and modules

Aktuelle Datei: 1 / 0

Part-time degree programme in German or English. The programme consists of 1500 hours (3 semesters) or 2250 hours (4 semesters). It is divided into a web-based foundation course, a web-based postgraduate course, seminars, internships and a Master's thesis. This corresponds to 60 ECTS (3 semesters) or 90 ECTS (4 semesters) points.

More information on curriculum

Admission to study programme

Admission requirements

Admission

1. general or relevant subject-linked higher education entrance qualification

2. proof of a national university degree in medicine (stomatology) or dentistry corresponding to at least 300 credits according to the ECTS standard.

3. The respective examination board decides on the determination of the admission requirements and in particular the equivalence of foreign degrees and other qualifications acquired abroad.

Note: The web-based basic modules ensure, after successful completion, that a uniform prerequisite also exists for participants from abroad for the specialisation modules.

Admission

Prospective students from non-EU countries please contact the International Admissions Team in the Enrollment Department.

 

Language requirements

The degree programme is offered in English and German. 
 

Costs

The participant is obliged to pay tuition fees of EUR 9,500.00/semester.
