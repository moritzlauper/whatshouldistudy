Study to change the world

You want to save the climate, feed the world and care about biodiversity? You live sustainability and see modern technologies in interaction with nature? Then BOKU University is the right place for you.

Our studies are as diverse as life itself. More than 40 studies enable you to participate in the shaping and future of our planet. Whether you are studying for a bachelor's, master's or doctorate degree, our programs range from biotechnology, nanotechnology and food technology to landscape, forestry and geotechnical engineering, chemistry, zoology and waste management. Immerse yourself in the fascinating world of science at BOKU University and find the education that will make your profession a vocation.

For prospective students

To make sure that you find the right study program for you, we recommend that you talk to our experts at BOKU4you or visit one of our information days.
